# V51 UI Design — 3D Professional System

V51 applies a controlled 3D visual system to the AI-Syed Jalal identity without turning the application into a heavy graphics demo.

## Design principles
- Depth comes from perspective, layered shadows, translucent surfaces and restrained motion.
- The AI-Syed Jalal logo remains the source asset; 3D treatment is applied through dimensional staging, orbital rings and lighting.
- Desktop pointer motion is limited to small rotations to protect usability and performance.
- Mobile devices receive simplified motion.
- `prefers-reduced-motion` disables non-essential animation.
- Existing navigation, data-entry, documents, AI and operations workflows remain unchanged.
