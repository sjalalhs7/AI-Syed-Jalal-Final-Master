AI vs GenAI vs AGI vs ASI vs OI
The Evolution of Intelligence

Five terms, five different ideas. Understand what exists today, what remains hypothetical, and how artificial intelligence differs from natural biological intelligence.

01 / ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE

AI

Task-focused machine intelligence

EXISTS TODAY

Definition: Computer systems that perform tasks associated with intelligence, such as recognizing patterns, making predictions, or selecting actions. They may use rules, learned models, or both.

Key characteristics

  • Often specialized for particular tasks
  • Can use rules or learned statistical patterns
  • Processes structured or unstructured data

Examples

Spam detectionRecommendationsFraud alertsAutomation
02 / GENERATIVE AI

GenAI

Content-generating artificial intelligence

EXISTS TODAY

Definition: AI systems that generate new content—such as text, images, audio, video, or code—based on patterns learned from training data and instructions.

Key characteristics

  • Creates new content from prompts or context
  • Learns patterns from large datasets
  • Can produce useful results but may also make mistakes

Examples

ChatGPTImage generationCode assistantsSpeech synthesis
03 / ARTIFICIAL GENERAL INTELLIGENCE

AGI

Broad, general-purpose machine intelligence

HYPOTHETICAL / NOT ESTABLISHED

Definition: A proposed AI capability that could learn, reason, and transfer knowledge across a broad range of intellectual tasks at a level comparable to humans. There is no universally agreed test for AGI.

Proposed characteristics

  • General-purpose learning across domains
  • Reasoning, planning, and adapting to new tasks
  • Transfer of knowledge beyond narrow training examples

Illustrative examples

Cross-domain problem solvingFlexible assistantsLearning unfamiliar tasks
04 / ARTIFICIAL SUPERINTELLIGENCE

ASI

Hypothetical intelligence beyond human capabilities

HYPOTHETICAL

Definition: A hypothetical form of AI that would substantially outperform humans across most or all important cognitive domains. Its arrival, design, and effects remain uncertain.

Hypothesized characteristics

  • Outperforms top human experts in many domains
  • Potentially exceptional scientific and strategic reasoning
  • Possible self-improvement, not a guaranteed feature

Illustrative possibilities

Scientific discoveryComplex optimizationGlobal challenges
05 / ORGANIC INTELLIGENCE

OI

Natural intelligence in living organisms

EXISTS TODAY

Definition: Intelligence arising from biological nervous systems and embodied living organisms, shaped by evolution, development, learning, and interaction with the environment.

Key characteristics

  • Biological and embodied
  • Learns from experience and sensory feedback
  • Adapts flexibly to changing real-world conditions

Examples

Human intelligenceAnimal cognitionNavigationSocial learning

Important distinction

These labels are not five proven stages on a single ladder. AI is the broad field; GenAI is a category within AI focused on generation. AGI and ASI describe hypothetical capability levels. OI describes naturally occurring biological intelligence, which is a different origin—not a future stage of AI.
