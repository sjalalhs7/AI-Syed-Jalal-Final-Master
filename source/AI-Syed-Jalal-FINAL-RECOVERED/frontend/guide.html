Use voice safely

  1. Open JalalAi and press Speak.
  2. Allow the microphone only if you want dictation.
  3. Review the transcript, then press Send.
  4. Press Read reply for browser speech, or Stop to cancel.

If your browser does not support speech recognition, keep typing. Browser speech may use your browser vendor’s service; it is not guaranteed to be on-device.

Cloud AI and external services

Local tools work now. Open-ended cloud answers need a server-side provider key and a successful live provider request. Configure keys only in your hosting provider’s secret settings—never paste them into chat or frontend files. See the deployment checklist.

Not connected: OpenJarvis desktop control, Gmail/Calendar, contacts, WhatsApp, scheduled briefings, purchases or autonomous external actions. The app will not pretend to perform them.

Premium ElevenLabs voice is separate from browser speech and is not enabled by default.

If something does not work

  • “Sign in”: open JalalAi and use “Try the local preview”, or sign in with your account.
  • Microphone blocked: allow microphone permission in browser/site settings, or type instead.
  • Cloud unavailable: use Local tools; the owner must configure and verify the provider.
  • Old screen after an update: reload once. Private pages and API responses are not cached.
  • Production use: do not use the shared preview for real data. Follow the deployment checklist and verify Neon/Cloudflare staging first.
