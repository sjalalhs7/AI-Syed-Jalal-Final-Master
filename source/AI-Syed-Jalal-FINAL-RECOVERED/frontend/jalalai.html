Privacy, permissions and connected services

Chat is transient and is not stored in AI history. Tasks and notes are saved only after your confirmation and are scoped to your account. This preview uses isolated local storage; production uses the configured server database. Cloud AI mode sends your message to the selected provider, subject to that provider’s terms. Browser speech recognition may process audio through your browser vendor; browser speech synthesis availability varies by device.

This page does not read Gmail, Calendar, contacts, files, or another person’s private data. No email, calendar, WhatsApp, sending, purchasing, deleting external data or computer-control action is connected. Future external actions require your approval and a separately verified integration.

ElevenLabs voice: optional premium playback is a separate integration and is not enabled by default. The Read reply button uses browser speech and does not require an ElevenLabs key.

WhatsApp: Not integrated. OpenJarvis desktop control: not connected to this website. Daily morning briefing: not a scheduled feature of this application.
