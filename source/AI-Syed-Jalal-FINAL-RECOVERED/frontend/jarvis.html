What this console does not do

  • It does not open doors, control your phone, send WhatsApp messages, read Gmail or run programs on your computer.
  • It does not listen in the background: the microphone is on only between “Hold to talk” and the final transcript.
  • It does not upload audio. The transcript is ordinary text you can edit before sending.
  • It does not fake a voice answer: if the browser exposes no voices, it says so.
