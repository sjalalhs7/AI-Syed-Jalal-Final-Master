Privacy and limitations
  • It does not open doors, control your phone, send WhatsApp messages, read Gmail or run programs on your computer.
  • There is no wake word or background listening. Conversation starts only when you choose it, retries once after silence, shows when the microphone is active, and stops when you leave or hide the tab. A stalled recognition session stops after 90 seconds without a speech update.
  • The application does not upload audio to its server. Your browser’s speech service may send audio to its provider, according to their terms.
  • It does not fake a voice answer: browser speech and installed voices vary by device. Type if recognition is unavailable.
  • Reports you choose to submit are stored for administrator review. Agent and research runs may save the prompt and trace to your account. Cloud requests send text to the selected provider; a key is not proof of a working connection.
