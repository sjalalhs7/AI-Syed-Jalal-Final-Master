How this arena is honest

What it really does

  • Runs each selected engine on the identical prompt and stores every answer.
  • Scores answers with a deterministic rubric (structure, specificity, coverage, honesty, code) and shows the sub-scores.
  • Hides engine names until you vote, then records whether you agreed with the judge.
  • Builds the leaderboard from your own decided battles only.

What it does not do

  • It does not claim your vote is objective, and the rubric judge is not a human reviewer — it is deterministic tooling whose scoring bands are printed on the page.
  • It does not invent results for engines with no configured key — those score zero and say why.
  • It does not hide engine identity cryptographically in blind mode: tone can still give it away.
  • It does not publish a global benchmark. Your ratings are yours.
