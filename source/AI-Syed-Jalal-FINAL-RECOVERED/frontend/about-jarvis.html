Meet JARVIS.

A voice and text assistant for calculations, tasks, notes and your AI-Syed Jalal workspace. Speak when you choose, review the response, and confirm before saving an action.

Talk or type

Use the text console or opt in to browser speech recognition. You can review dictated words before sending them.

Useful local tools

Calculate, review tasks and find notes with bounded tools; adding a task or note requires your confirmation.

Optional cloud AI

Choose a configured provider for open-ended answers. Its availability and terms depend on that provider.

What to know

  • A workspace account and working application server are required.
  • Speech recognition and voices depend on the browser and device. The browser's speech service may process audio online.
  • Cloud requests send text to the selected provider; conversation context is optional.
  • JARVIS does not control your computer, send messages or connect to your email or calendar.
