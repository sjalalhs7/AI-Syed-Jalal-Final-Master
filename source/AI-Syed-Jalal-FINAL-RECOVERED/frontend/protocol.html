The app never opens the link for you. Opening a link another person sent is your decision, made in your own browser.

How this works

Any page can link to web+ajalal://… once this app is installed and chosen as the handler for that scheme. The manifest declares the handler; the operating system owns the choice.

A protocol handler is a convenience, not a trust signal: treat the link like any other link.
