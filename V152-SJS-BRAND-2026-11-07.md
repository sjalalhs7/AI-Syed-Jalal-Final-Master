# V152 — The SJS identity

Date: 7 November 2026 · Base: `main` @ `e23eead` (V151) · Repo: `sjalalhs7/AI-Syed-Jalal-Final-Master`

## The mark

An **SJS** monogram — gold, to orange, to emerald, on the approved deep navy —
inside orbital rings, with a sparkle node reading as the agent's "live" point.
Built as a design system rather than one picture, because one picture cannot
survive every place a logo is used.

| Variant | File | Why it exists |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Primary | `assets/brand/sjs-logo.svg` | Headers, sign-in, app tile |
| Mark only | `assets/brand/sjs-mark.svg` | For surfaces that supply their own background |
| Maskable | `assets/brand/sjs-maskable.svg` | Android crops to a circle; art stays inside the 80% safe zone |
| Compact | `assets/brand/sjs-compact.svg` | 16–48 px. Orbits and sparkles are **dropped**, letters enlarged |
| Mono | `assets/brand/sjs-mono.svg` | One colour, for print, embossing and stamping |
| Favicon | `assets/brand/favicon.svg` | Browser tab |
| Social | `assets/brand/sjs-og-1200x630.png` | Open Graph / Twitter card |

Raster exports: 16, 32, 48, 180 (Apple touch), 192, 512 and maskable 512.

**Why the letters are real type.** The first iterations drew the strokes by
hand and read as soft and amateur. The shipped wordmark is **Montserrat Black**,
converted to outlines at export, so it is typographically correct at any size and
needs no font file on the device.

**Why there are two cuts.** At 16 px the orbital rings and the sparkles collapse
into noise. The compact cut removes them and enlarges the letters — that
simplification is what keeps the favicon legible, and a test enforces it.

## The design refresh

The crown-era UI carried violet and pink accents that fought the gold/emerald
mark. Every decorative accent was remapped onto the brand ramp: headline
gradients, the command-centre preview border and glow, the login card, the nav
active tint and the avatar chip. The headline now runs
`#fff4c2 → #ffd15a → #ff9500 → #38e08c`, which is the mark's own gradient.

Two accessibility fixes came out of the audit:

- The primary button inherited the page's gradient `-webkit-background-clip:text`
  rule, so "Open Agent Studio →" rendered as pale yellow on yellow. It now sets
  its own colour.
- The header mark was 44 px, at which "SJS" was unreadable. The lockup is now
  56 px on desktop / 46 px on mobile.

The approved layout, hierarchy and theme language are untouched. Only colour
accents, the mark and its sizing changed — no restructuring.

## What was replaced

The crowned-SJ rainbow logo is retired from every page, the manifest, the
favicons and the social card. `assets/approved-sj-rainbow-crown.svg` and
`assets/ai-syed-jalal-social-card.jpg` remain on disk so old links and cached
documents do not break, but nothing loads them.

## Gates

`server/tests/brand-v152.test.mjs` (6 checks) fails the build if:

1. A brand SVG is not a 512 viewBox, or loses its accessible `<title>`.
2. The monochrome cut starts depending on a gradient.
3. Any of the 24 pages loses the SJS mark, the SVG favicon or the Apple touch
   icon — including the 404 page and the redirect stubs.
4. A declared icon size disagrees with the PNG's real pixel dimensions.
5. The compact cut regains the orbits that make small sizes unreadable.
6. The social card is not 1200×630, or the homepage points OG tags at the
   retired JPEG.

Full pass at V152: **148 server · 80 frontend · 51 static · 50 browser**, all
green, `23 of 23` schema tables from an empty database, and a service worker that
installs, caches 30 entries and serves a page offline.

## Not changed

- The wordmark inside the manifest is still "AI-Syed Jalal"; only the mark is SJS.
- The four themes (glass, water, fire, rainbow) are untouched; "rainbowmorphism"
  keeps its multicolour treatment on purpose — it is a theme, not the brand.

## Verified how

Every claim above came from a render or a test, not a mockup: the icons were
rendered from the shipped SVGs at their real sizes and inspected, the pages were
screenshotted in Chromium at 1440 px and 414 px, and the archives in `dist/` were
extracted to a clean directory and re-tested before being handed over.
